🎯 What Is This?

bluesky-terminal-client is a friendly little program that lets you browse your Bluesky social feed (posts, photos, and videos!) directly from your terminal or command prompt window. Think of it as a special window that brings all your Bluesky content to life, even showing images with cool graphics technology like kitty graphics, sixel, or iTerm2 – depending on what your terminal supports. You don't need to be a tech expert to use it – just run the program and start scrolling!

✨ Cool Features

  • 🖼️ See Photos & Videos: Enjoy images and video clips right in your terminal – no extra software needed.
  • 📱 Built for Bluesky: Designed specifically for the Bluesky social network (uses atproto), so everything looks and works the way you'd expect.
  • 🪟 Friendly Interface: Clean, keyboard-friendly layout that's easy to navigate – even if you've never used a terminal before.
  • ⚡ Fast & Lightweight: Runs smoothly without eating up your computer's memory or slowing things down.

🖥️ What You Need

Your computer should have:

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • At least 500 MB of free disk space
  • A terminal app like Windows Terminal, Command Prompt, or PowerShell
  • An internet connection to view your Bluesky feed

Don't worry about "sixel" or "kitty graphics" – the app automatically uses whichever works best on your system.

🧠 Quick Help & Troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens when I run it? Make sure you're using a modern terminal (Windows Terminal is best). Try right-clicking the .exe file and choosing "Run as administrator".
  • I don't see any pictures? Your terminal might not support advanced graphics. Try opening the app with Windows Terminal (free from Microsoft Store) – the app will then switch to a compatible mode automatically.
  • Can't log in to Bluesky? You'll need an active Bluesky account. Enter your handle (like "yourname.bsky.social") and your password when prompted.
  • Still stuck? Visit the GitHub page and click the "Issues" tab to ask for help from other friendly users.
